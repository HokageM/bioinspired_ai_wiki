# Uncanny valley

**Named, not defined.** The only appearance in the module:

> [[nico]]'s head: **child-like appearance ? avoids uncanny-valley effect**

That is the entire content. The lecture assumes the reader knows what the effect
is, what shape the "valley" has, and why child-like proportions avoid it.

This page exists because the term is doing real design work ? it is the stated
reason for a physical design decision on the platform ? while carrying no
definition the wiki can cite.

## What the source does establish

Only the **inference**: *if* the uncanny valley is a hazard, *then* making a
robot look like a child rather than like an adult human is a way around it. The
implied mechanism is that a stylised, clearly non-adult form sets expectations
low enough that small imperfections do not read as wrong.

## What the source does not establish

- What the effect is, or whose it is
- What is on the axes
- Whether it is about appearance, motion, or both
- Any evidence that NICO in fact avoids it
