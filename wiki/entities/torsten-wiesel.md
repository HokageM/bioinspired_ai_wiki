# Torsten Wiesel

Named in [[L06-hierarchical-vision]] p4 as one half of "**Hubel and Wiesel**",
alongside [[david-hubel]]. The notes treat the pair as a single unit and
attribute everything jointly.

## What the source says

Nothing about Wiesel individually. The joint attribution covers:

- Cells in the striate cortex respond best to **bars of light**, not spots.
- The simple / complex cell distinction — see
  [[simple-complex-hypercomplex-cells]].
- **[[orientation-tuning]]** and its falloff curve.

No first name, dates, institution, or publication appear in the notes.

## Why the work matters to the module

See [[david-hubel]] for the full argument. In short: this is the measurement
from which [[kunihiko-fukushima]]'s [[neocognitron]] and then
[[yann-lecun]]'s [[convolutional-network]] were derived — the only place in the
module where a documented chain runs from a neurophysiological recording to a
working architecture, rather than an analogy being asserted after the fact.
See [[ann-brain-correspondence]].
