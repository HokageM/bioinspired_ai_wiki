# Teuvo Kohonen

> [!info] Named only by attribution
> The notes never write his name. They write **"Kohonen map"** and
> **"distorted Kohonen map"**. This page exists because the eponym is used as a
> technical term throughout [[self-organising-map]] and readers of the wiki will
> want to know who it refers to. Everything below is `[external]`.

## What the lecture uses the name for

- **Kohonen map** — the grid formulation of the SOM: an *m*-dimensional grid of
  units `u`, each with an *n*-dimensional weight vector `w`.
- **Distorted Kohonen map** — the visualisation of the trained map, where
  distance between grid units reflects distance in the high-dimensional space.

## `[external]`

Finnish engineer (1934–2021), who introduced the self-organising map in the
early 1980s. The SOM is his best-known contribution and the reason the terms
*Kohonen map* and *self-organising map* are used interchangeably.
