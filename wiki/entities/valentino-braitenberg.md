# Valentino Braitenberg

Named only via **"Braitenberg Vehicles"** in
[[L08-behaviour-based-robotics]]. No first name, date, or citation.
[external] The first name here is supplied by the wiki, not the source.

## What the module attributes to him

The [[braitenberg-vehicle]]s: two-sensor, two-actuator agents whose entire
behavioural repertoire is set by **wiring topology** (straight or crossed) and
**sign** (excitatory or inhibitory) ?

| | Excitatory | Inhibitory |
|---|---|---|
| **Straight** | Fear | Admires |
| **Crossed** | Aggression | Explores |

? and with them the accompanying claim that such an agent's behaviour is
*goal-directed, fast, flexible, adaptive*, and **might even appear intelligent**,
with **no cognitive processes** whatever.

## Why the vehicles are an argument, not a demo

They are a **constructive refutation**: given an agent whose behaviour supports
every mentalistic description we ordinarily use, and whose mechanism contains
nothing but four wires, the inference from behaviour to mechanism is unsound.

This bears directly on [[intelligent-behaviour]], the question L01 opened with,
and on the [[ann-brain-correspondence]] thread: it is a warning that *behavioural
similarity is cheap*. L07 then makes the opposite case ? that reproducing
**fine-grained measured signatures** is not cheap. Both are right, and the
distinction between them is the module's most useful methodological point.
