# iCub

A humanoid robot platform, named once in [[L09-bio-inspired-attention]] as the
**instructor** in the human?human?robot collaboration study:

> **H1: actor** (arm move) ? **H2: guide** (arm move) ?
> **Instructor: iCub** ? **gaze shift, verbal instructions**

## What the source establishes

Only its **capabilities in this task**: it can shift gaze in a way humans read,
and it can give verbal instructions. Both are manipulated experimentally, and the
reported effects are on [[social-attention]]:

> **Facial expressions & eye gaze ? completion time and ratings of the robot's
> intelligence.** Robot happy face ? faster completion; initial gaze to the guide
> ? rated more intelligent; gaze familiarity increased performance and perception.

So iCub is used here as an **attention-directing device** rather than as a
manipulator ? the opposite of [[nico]]'s role in L08.
