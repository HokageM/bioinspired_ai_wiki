# NAO

A humanoid robot platform, named once in [[L09-bio-inspired-attention]]:

> with different personalities (intro / extro) and different autonomy (WoZ).**

That is the entire content. No description, no sensors, no size, no maker.

## What the source establishes

Only its **role**: NAO is the body in which the modelled social cues of human
assistants were implemented and tested. See [[human-robot-collaboration]].
